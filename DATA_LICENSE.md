# Data license

The curated data files under `data/` (the three-state NMR population records
and their provenance files) are released under the **Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International** license (CC-BY-4.0).

- Human-readable deed: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- Full legal code: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode>

Reuse requires appropriate credit to the paper and its authors, a link to the
license, and an indication of any changes made. Until the double-blind review
concludes, credit the accompanying anonymous submission rather than naming
individuals.

Everything else in this repository (source code, analysis scripts, generated
result files that are not curated data) is licensed under the MIT License in
`LICENSE`. Generated synthetic datasets remain covered by the MIT License.
